Refusal-risk review

Most refusals do not happen because people are not genuine. They happen because the application does not prove the case clearly enough.

Axis Visa looks for the gaps, inconsistencies and missing explanations that can make a genuine trip appear unclear.

Unclear travel purpose

The visit reason, itinerary or host relationship does not connect cleanly.

Weak evidence of ties

Work, family, study, property or return reasons are not properly evidenced.

Confusing finances

Deposits, income patterns or sponsorship are present but not clearly explained.

Missing documents

Important proof is absent, outdated or not matched to the application facts.

Personal circumstances

Self-employment, informal income or residence history needs a stronger narrative.

Previous refusals

Earlier decisions are not addressed directly enough before reapplying.

Refund structure

Clear pricing. Clear refund rules.

The £109 full-support fee is split into a £35 admin fee and a £74 service fee. The service fee is conditionally refundable if a pre-approved application is refused.

Refunds do not apply where:

  • The client provided false or incomplete information.
  • The client submitted without following agreed guidance.
  • The refusal was caused by undisclosed facts.
  • The application was not pre-approved for the refund structure.
  • The client used the consultation only.

Compliance and boundaries

What Axis Visa does - and what we do not do.

Axis Visa provides visa-readiness support, document organisation and application preparation assistance. We are not a government body, embassy, consulate, law firm or immigration tribunal representative. We do not guarantee visa approval. Where regulated immigration advice, legal representation or appeal advice is required, clients should seek assistance from an appropriately authorised adviser or legal professional.
